## About This Song
"Oh, Pretty Woman" was written by Roy Orbison and Bill Dees and released in 1964. The idea for the title came from Orbison's wife Claudette. She interrupted one of their conversations to say she was heading out and the moment sparked the song. It went on to reach number one on the Billboard Hot 100.

## First Impressions
That opening guitar riff grabs you right away and Roy's voice carries the rest.

## My Reaction
"Oh, Pretty Woman" is simple, catchy and full of personality. Knowing it came from a quick moment with his wife makes it even better. The growl and the "mercy" are pure Roy Orbison.
