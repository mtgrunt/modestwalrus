## About This Song
"Con te partirò," Italian for "With You I Shall Depart," was written by Francesco Sartori, who composed the music, and Lucio Quarantotto, who wrote the lyrics. Italian tenor Andrea Bocelli first performed it at the 1995 Sanremo Music Festival and recorded it that same year for his album Bocelli. Released as a single alongside "Vivere," it went to number one in France, where it became one of the country's best-selling singles ever, and then in Belgium, where it set a new all-time sales record.

In 1996 a new version sung partly in English was released as "Time to Say Goodbye," pairing Bocelli with British soprano Sarah Brightman. It was an even bigger success, topping charts across Europe and becoming the best-selling single in German history. A version with Brightman singing in German and Bocelli in Italian appears on the CD Time to Say Goodbye and that version alone has sold more than 12 million copies worldwide, placing it among the best-selling singles of all time. The song also has a Spanish version, "Por ti volaré," meaning "For You I Shall Fly."

## My Reaction
"Time To Say Goodbye" is breathtaking. Bocelli's warm tenor and Brightman's crystal-clear soprano blend perfectly and the song keeps building toward an emotional peak that gives you chills. It's easy to see why it has been played at so many farewells and celebrations.
