## About This Song
"Bleed It Out" is a song by American rock band Linkin Park, released on August 17, 2007 as the second single from their third studio album, Minutes to Midnight. Produced by Rick Rubin and Mike Shinoda, it pairs Shinoda's rapid-fire verses with Chester Bennington's explosive chorus over pounding drums and handclaps.

## First Impressions
The drums and handclaps kick in fast and the song never lets up from there.

## My Reaction
"Bleed It Out" is Linkin Park letting loose. Mike Shinoda's verses are sharp and urgent and Chester Bennington's screams give the chorus incredible power. It's loud, messy and full of energy in the best way.
