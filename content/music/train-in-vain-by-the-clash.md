## About This Song
"Train in Vain," also known as "Train in Vain (Stand by Me)," is a song by English punk rock band The Clash. It closes their third studio album, London Calling (1979), but it wasn't printed on the original track listing, so it played as a hidden track at the end of the record. It was later released as the album's third and final single. Mick Jones sings lead on it.

## First Impressions
The driving beat kicks in right away and Mick Jones' vocals make it instantly catchy.

## My Reaction
"Train in Vain" shows a poppier, more heartfelt side of The Clash. It's a breakup song with real bite and a groove that's hard to shake. Saving it as a surprise at the end of London Calling was the perfect finishing touch.
