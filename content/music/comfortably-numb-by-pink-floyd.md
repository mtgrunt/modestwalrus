## About This Song
"Comfortably Numb" appears on The Wall, Pink Floyd's 1979 rock opera. David Gilmour wrote the music and Roger Waters wrote the lyrics, with Waters singing the verses and Gilmour taking the chorus. The song is best known for Gilmour's two guitar solos, especially the soaring final one, which regularly ranks among the greatest solos in rock history.

## First Impressions
It starts slow and dreamlike, then builds toward something huge.

## My Reaction
"Comfortably Numb" is Pink Floyd at their most powerful. The contrast between Waters' cold, detached verses and Gilmour's warm chorus is striking. Then the final solo arrives and every note feels like it means something. It's the kind of solo you never get tired of hearing.
