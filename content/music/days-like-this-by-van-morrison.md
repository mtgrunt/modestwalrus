## About This Song
"Days Like This" is the title song of Van Morrison's 1995 album Days Like This, released as a single in May 1995 on Mercury Records. Morrison wrote and produced it and Brian Kennedy sings backing vocals. It became the official anthem of the peace movement in Northern Ireland and Morrison performed it for a huge crowd in Belfast when President Bill Clinton visited on 30 November 1995. The song also appears on the soundtrack of the 1997 film As Good as It Gets.

## First Impressions
The easygoing groove and bright horns make it feel like a sunny afternoon right from the start.

## My Reaction
"Days Like This" is simple, joyful and full of heart. Van Morrison's voice carries a quiet confidence that makes the message feel earned and the horns give it a lift that's hard to resist. It's no surprise this song came to stand for hope in Northern Ireland.
