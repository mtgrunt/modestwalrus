## About This Song
"Zombie" was released in 1994 on The Cranberries' second album, *No Need to Argue*. Written by Dolores O'Riordan in response to the 1993 Warrington bombing during the Troubles. It's a protest song about violence and loss rather than a typical single for the band and it became their biggest international hit.

## First Impressions
Dolores O'Riordan's voice carries an intensity live that recordings only hint at. The crowd at Bercy is fully with her from the first note.

## My Reaction
What a stunning performance. O'Riordan's delivery is raw and commanding and the band behind her. Noel Hogan, Mike Hogan and Fergal Lawler, gives the song a weight that fills the arena. It's a reminder of how much force "Zombie" carries as a live piece, well beyond what the studio version captures.
