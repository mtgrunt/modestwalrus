## About This Song
"Sunday Morning" appears on Tragic Kingdom, No Doubt's 1995 breakthrough album, and was released as a single in 1997. It was written by Gwen Stefani, bassist Tony Kanal and Gwen's brother Eric Stefani. The song mixes ska rhythms with rock guitars and Gwen's punchy, playful vocals.

## First Impressions
The bouncy groove kicks in fast and Gwen's voice cuts right through it.

## My Reaction
"Sunday Morning" is a perfect slice of 90s No Doubt. It's catchy and full of attitude, with Tony Kanal's bass driving the whole thing forward. The energy shifts between laid-back and explosive and it never stops being fun.
