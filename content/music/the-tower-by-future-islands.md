## About This Song
"The Tower" is a track by Future Islands, the Baltimore synth-pop band fronted by Samuel T. Herring. It's built on warm, layered synths and a steady. Pulsing rhythm, with Herring's voice carrying the emotion front and center.

## First Impressions
The synths wash over you right away, warm and unhurried. Herring's vocals feel intimate, as if he's singing directly to you.

## My Reaction
Future Islands songs are like a massage for the soul. The music is patient and generous, never rushing to make a point and Herring's delivery is so earnest that it's hard not to feel lighter by the end. "The Tower" captures that feeling well, a soothing, heartfelt track that's easy to return to whenever you need to slow down.
