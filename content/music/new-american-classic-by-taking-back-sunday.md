## About This Song
"New American Classic" appears on Louder Now, Taking Back Sunday's 2006 album. The guitar uses open G tuning (D G D G B D from low to high), which lets open strings ring out under the chords and gives the song its full, warm sound.

## First Impressions
It's more laid back than most of the album, with ringing guitars that set a reflective mood right away.

## My Reaction
"New American Classic" shows Taking Back Sunday can slow things down without losing any emotional weight. The open G tuning gives the guitars a rich, resonant tone and the vocals sit perfectly on top of it.
