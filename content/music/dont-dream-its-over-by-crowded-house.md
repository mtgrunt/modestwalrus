## Why I Reacted To This Song
Some songs try to solve you. This one just sits beside you instead and somehow that's the more honest gesture.

## About This Song
"Don't Dream It's Over" was written by Neil Finn and appeared on Crowded House's self-titled 1986 debut album. It became the band's signature song and biggest commercial hit, reaching number two on the US Billboard Hot 100 in early 1987. Decades on, it's held up as a kind of enduring comfort anthem, returned to again and again on radio and in tribute performances whenever people need something gentle to hold onto.

## First Impressions
The melody arrives unhurried and warm, like it already knows you don't need convincing, just company.

## My Reaction
It does not try to fix the faulty machinery of reality, it just offers a hand to hold while the wheels in motion turn. That's the whole appeal and it's rarer than it should be. So much of what gets called a "comfort song" oversells its own remedy, promising the ache will lift if you just believe hard enough. This one never makes that promise. It simply stays present through the difficulty, which ends up feeling far more trustworthy than any tidy resolution could.
