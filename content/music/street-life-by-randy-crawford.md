## About This Song
"Street Life" was released in 1979 by jazz funk group The Crusaders, with Randy Crawford on vocals, as the title track of their album Street Life. It was written by the band's keyboardist Joe Sample with lyricist Will Jennings. The song became a major hit and turned Crawford into a star in her own right.

## First Impressions

## My Reaction
"Street Life" is cool, classy and full of feeling. The band's jazzy groove is effortless and Randy Crawford sings with real power and warmth. It paints a vivid picture of life on the city streets and it still sounds fantastic today.
