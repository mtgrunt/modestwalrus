## About This Song
"Teenage Dirtbag" is a song by American rock band Wheatus, released on June 20, 2000 as the band's debut single and the lead single from their self-titled first album. Guitarist and singer Brendan B. Brown wrote it based on his own childhood. In the summer of 1984, when Brown was 10 years old and living on Long Island, a teenager named Ricky Kasso committed a widely publicized murder in the woods behind his house. Kasso was arrested wearing an AC/DC T-shirt and that moment stuck with Brown, shaping how kids who loved hard rock were seen and eventually inspiring the song.

## My Reaction
"Teenage Dirtbag" captures what it feels like to be the kid nobody notices. Brendan Brown's high, cracking vocals make it awkward and sincere at the same time and the chorus is a guaranteed singalong. Knowing the darker story behind it adds a whole new layer.
