## About This Song
"Johnny B. Goode" was released in 1958 by Chuck Berry, built around one of the most influential guitar intros in rock history. Telling the story of a poor country boy who becomes a guitar star. It became a blueprint that countless rock and roll songs would follow and remains one of the genre's defining records.

## First Impressions
That opening guitar lick is instantly recognizable, and the energy never lets up from there. It's easy to hear exactly why this riff became a template for an entire genre.

## My Reaction
Berry's guitar work here is the whole story: sharp, driving and endlessly replicated since. The song moves with a swagger that still feels fresh and it's clear why so many guitarists point back to this record as a starting point. "Johnny B. Goode" earns its place as one of the cornerstones of rock and roll.
