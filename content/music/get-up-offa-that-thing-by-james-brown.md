## About This Song
James Brown wrote and recorded "Get Up Offa That Thing" and released it in 1976 as a two-part single, with the B-side "Release the Pressure" carrying the same song forward. It climbed to number 4 on the R&B chart and number 45 on the Billboard Hot 100, putting Brown back in the R&B Top Ten after a year away. That success made it his biggest hit of the late 1970s. The message is simple: get up and dance until you feel better. Because of his trouble with the IRS over unpaid back taxes, Brown credited the songwriting to his wife Deidre and their daughters, Deanna and Yamma Brown.
