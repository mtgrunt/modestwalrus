## About This Song
"Take Me Out" was released in 2004 on Franz Ferdinand's self-titled debut album. The Glasgow band built the song around a famous switch in tempo: it opens fast and frantic before slowing down into the stomping guitar riff that made it a hit. Its music video uses a striking collage style of animation that matches the song's sharp, angular sound.

## First Impressions
The song kicks off at full speed, then drops into that riff and it's impossible not to move.

## My Reaction
"Take Me Out" is one of the defining indie rock songs of the 2000s. The tempo change is a brilliant hook and Alex Kapranos' vocals are cool and confident. Paired with that unique video, it's a song that still feels fresh every time.
