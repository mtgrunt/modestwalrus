## Why I Reacted To This Song
This is one of the unforgettable hits of the 1960s and it still feels as warm and immediate as the day it was recorded.

## About This Song
"Stand By Me" was released in 1961 by Ben E. King, written by King alongside Jerry Leiber and Mike Stoller. Built around a gentle, steady groove and King's tender lead vocal, it became a defining soul record of its era and has since been covered and reused countless times.

## First Impressions
The song opens with that unmistakable bassline and King's voice comes in close and sincere. It's simple in the best way: nothing is overdone and nothing needs to be.

## My Reaction
What makes "Stand By Me" last is how little it asks of you. King sings with complete sincerity and the arrangement stays out of his way, letting the promise at the heart of the song speak for itself. I don't think Ben E. King ever imagined that a record made in 1961 would still be stuck in our heads this many decades on, but it's exactly the kind of song that earns that kind of staying power.
