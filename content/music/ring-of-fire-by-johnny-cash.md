## About This Song
"Ring of Fire" was written by June Carter and Merle Kilgore and first recorded by June's sister, Anita Carter. Johnny Cash released his own version in 1963, adding the bright mariachi horns that became the song's signature. It spent weeks at number one on the Billboard country chart and became one of the most famous songs of his career. The song is often seen as June's way of describing falling for Cash, whom she would later marry.

## First Impressions

## My Reaction
"Ring of Fire" is Johnny Cash at his most iconic. The horns give it a sound unlike anything else in country music and his voice carries all the weight of falling hard for someone. Decades later it's still impossible not to sing along.
