## About This Song
"Respect" was released in 1967 on Aretha Franklin's album *I Never Loved a Man the Way I Love You*. Originally written and recorded by Otis Redding in 1965, Franklin's version reworked the song into something sharper and more defiant. Turning it into an anthem for self-respect that resonated far beyond its original context.

## First Impressions
Franklin takes command from the first line. There's no pleading in her voice, just a flat demand that still feels bracing decades later.

## My Reaction
What makes "Respect" land is how plainly it states its terms. There's no negotiating, no softening the request; it's simply spelled out, letter by letter. That directness is exactly what so many adults still need reminding of: respect isn't something you earn by staying quiet and hoping, it's something you ask for clearly and expect to get. Franklin delivers that lesson better than almost anyone ever has.
