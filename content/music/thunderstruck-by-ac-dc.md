## About This Song
"Thunderstruck" is a song by Australian hard rock band AC/DC and the lead single from their twelfth studio album, The Razors Edge (1990). It reached number 4 on Australia's ARIA Singles Chart, number 1 in Finland and number 5 on the US Billboard Album Rock Tracks chart. In 2010 it was named the top song in Triple M Melbourne's Ultimate 500 Rock Countdown. With more than 15 million units sold, it ranks among the best-selling singles of all time.
