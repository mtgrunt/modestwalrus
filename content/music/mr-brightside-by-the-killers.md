## About This Song
"Mr. Brightside" is the breakout hit from Hot Fuss, the 2004 debut album by Las Vegas band The Killers. Written by singer Brandon Flowers and guitarist Dave Keuning, it tells the story of a man consumed by jealousy as he imagines his partner with someone else. It has spent an incredibly long run on the UK charts and remains one of the most beloved singalongs of the 2000s.

## First Impressions
That racing guitar riff kicks in and the song takes off at full speed.

## My Reaction
"Mr. Brightside" never slows down. Brandon Flowers sings every line with nervous, desperate energy and the chorus is impossible not to shout along to. Years later it still gets everyone singing the second it comes on.
