## About This Song
"The Warmth" appears on Incubus's 1999 album Make Yourself, the record that pushed the band away from their earlier funk-metal leanings toward something more melodic and atmospheric. The track opens with whale song samples before settling into a heavy, rolling bass line courtesy of Dirk Lance, with Brandon Boyd's vocals sitting on top of the groove rather than fighting it.

## First Impressions
Those opening whale calls throw you off for a second, then the bass drops in and the whole thing locks into place.

## My Reaction
The message is simple and it works: the world is cold and full of ignorance, so hold onto the wonderful things and the good people while you can. That's not a complicated idea, but the song sells it through feel rather than lyrics alone. The bass line is the real anchor here, it hits harder than anything else on the track and carries the warmth the title promises. It's not flashy, it just settles into a groove and stays there, which is exactly the point.
