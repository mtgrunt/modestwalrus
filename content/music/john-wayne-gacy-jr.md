## About This Song
"John Wayne Gacy, Jr." appears on Sufjan Stevens' 2005 album Illinois. The song tells the story of the Chicago-area serial killer through soft vocals and gentle acoustic guitar. The guitar is in standard tuning (E A D G B E) with a capo on the 3rd fret, fingerpicking Dm, Cadd9, Am and F shapes that sound in the key of E flat. It ends with Stevens turning the lens on himself, admitting that in his best behavior he is really just like him.

## First Impressions
It's quiet and delicate from the first note, which makes the subject matter hit even harder.

## My Reaction
"John Wayne Gacy, Jr." shouldn't work as well as it does. Stevens tells a horrifying story with tenderness instead of shock and the contrast is what makes it so unsettling. The final lines leave you thinking long after the song ends.
