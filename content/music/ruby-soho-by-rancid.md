## About This Song
"Ruby Soho" appears on ...And Out Come the Wolves, Rancid's 1995 album released on Epitaph Records. Tim Armstrong and Lars Frederiksen trade vocals over a bouncy, ska-flavored groove, with Matt Freeman's bass driving it forward. It became one of the band's best known songs.

## First Impressions
The upbeat rhythm kicks in right away and the chorus hooks you on the first listen.

## My Reaction
"Ruby Soho" is catchy, rough around the edges and full of heart. Tim Armstrong's gravelly voice gives it character and the singalong chorus makes it impossible to forget. It's a perfect snapshot of 90s punk.
