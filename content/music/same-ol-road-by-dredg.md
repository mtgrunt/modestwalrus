## About This Song
"Same Ol' Road" is the second track on El Cielo, the concept album Dredg released on 8 October, 2002 through Interscope Records. The album was inspired by Salvador Dalí's painting "Dream Caused by the Flight of a Bee Around a Pomegranate a Second Before Awakening," which the band has linked to sleep paralysis. "Same Ol' Road" was one of two songs from the album that got a music video, directed by American McGee. The band is Gavin Hayes on vocals and guitar, Mark Engles on guitar, Drew Roulette on bass and Dino Campanella on drums and keys.

## First Impressions
The layered guitars and Gavin Hayes' soaring vocals create a big, dreamy sound right away.

## My Reaction
"Same Ol' Road" shows how good Dredg is at building atmosphere. The song moves between quiet and powerful moments without ever losing its pull and Hayes' voice ties it all together. It's a great entry point into one of rock's most underrated bands.
