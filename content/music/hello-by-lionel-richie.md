## About This Song
"Hello" appears on Can't Slow Down, Lionel Richie's 1983 album on Motown, and was released as a single in 1984. The ballad went to number one on the Billboard Hot 100 and the UK Singles Chart. Its music video, which follows a teacher who falls for a blind art student as she sculpts a clay bust of him, became one of the most talked-about videos of the decade.

## First Impressions
The gentle piano and Lionel's soft, tender voice draw you in from the very first line.

## My Reaction
"Hello" is simple, sincere and beautifully sung. Lionel Richie pours real longing into every line and the melody stays with you long after it ends. It's a classic ballad that still holds up today.
