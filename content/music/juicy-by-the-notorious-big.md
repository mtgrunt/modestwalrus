## Why I Reacted To This Song
I miss when rappers used to talk about life, not lifestyle. This is the one that proves it.

## About This Song
"Juicy" was released in 1994 as a single from The Notorious B.I.G.'s debut album, *Ready to Die*. Built over a sample of Mtume's "Juicy Fruit," it tells Biggie's story of growing up poor in Brooklyn, dreaming of success and finally reaching it. On top of that, reflecting on how far he'd come rather than how much he'd bought.

## First Impressions
The beat is warm and unhurried and Biggie's delivery sounds relaxed but deliberate. Every line feels like it comes from lived experience rather than a pose.

## My Reaction
Biggie doesn't brag about the car or the jewelry for its own sake. He tells you where he came from, what he had to do to get out and what it actually felt like when the dream came true. That's what makes it hit: it's about life, with the lifestyle as the reward rather than the point. It's a reminder of what rap can do when it's honest, and it's why this record still holds up.
