## About This Song
"Suspicious Minds" was written by Mark James and recorded by Elvis Presley in 1969 at American Sound Studio in Memphis with producer Chips Moman. It became his final number one hit on the Billboard Hot 100.

Elvis Aaron Presley was born January 8, 1935 and died August 16, 1977. Known as the "King of Rock and Roll," the American singer and actor is widely considered one of the most culturally important figures of the 20th century. His energetic, provocative stage presence and his blend of influences from across racial lines arrived during a time of major change in race relations, bringing him huge success along with early controversy.

## My Reaction
"Suspicious Minds" shows Elvis at his most passionate. The song is about a relationship falling apart and he sings it with real heartbreak and urgency. The fake fade-out near the end that comes roaring back is one of the best moments in any of his songs.
