## About This Song
"Monophobia" is a 2018 collaboration between deadmau5 and Rob Swire, the frontman of Pendulum and co-founder of Knife Party. It was released on deadmau5's label mau5trap and appeared on the EP mau5ville: Level 1. The title means the fear of being alone and Swire's haunting vocals carry that feeling over deadmau5's dark, pulsing production.

## First Impressions
The brooding synths set the tone right away and Rob Swire's voice fits the mood perfectly.

## My Reaction
"Monophobia" brings together two of electronic music's biggest names and it delivers. deadmau5's slow-burning production gives the song room to breathe and Swire's vocals add a human, emotional edge. It's dark, polished and easy to get lost in.
