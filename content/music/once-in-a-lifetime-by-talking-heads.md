## About This Song
"Once in a Lifetime" appears on Remain in Light, Talking Heads' 1980 album produced by Brian Eno, and was released as a single in 1981. Built on layered, looping grooves inspired by African rhythms, it features David Byrne delivering the verses like a fiery preacher. The music video, co-directed by Byrne and Toni Basil, shows off his famously jerky, twitching dance moves.

## First Impressions
The hypnotic groove pulls you in and Byrne's frantic, sermon-like delivery keeps you hooked.

## My Reaction
"Once in a Lifetime" is strange in the best way. It asks big questions about how we end up where we are in life, all over a groove you can't help but move to. Byrne's performance in the video is unforgettable and the song still feels as fresh and unusual as it did in 1980.
