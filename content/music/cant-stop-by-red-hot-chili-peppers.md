## About This Song
"Can't Stop" appears on By the Way, Red Hot Chili Peppers' 2002 album produced by Rick Rubin, and was released as a single in 2003. It's driven by John Frusciante's choppy, funky guitar riff, Flea's bass and Chad Smith's drums, with Anthony Kiedis delivering rapid-fire verses over the top. Its music video, directed by Mark Romanek, was inspired by artist Erwin Wurm's One Minute Sculptures.
