## About This Song
"Take on Me" is from Hunting High and Low, the 1985 debut album by Norwegian band a-ha, and was written by Pål Waaktaar, Magne Furuholmen and Morten Harket. The first version flopped in the UK in 1984, so the band re-recorded it with producer Alan Tarney. Even that version took three UK releases before it finally caught on. It reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100 in October 1985 and number 2 in the UK. The video, directed by Steve Barron, blends live action with pencil-sketch animation using a technique called rotoscoping, where live footage is traced frame by frame. Animators Michael Patterson and Candace Reckinger rotoscoped around 3,000 frames over 16 weeks. The video won six MTV Video Music Awards in 1986 and in 2024 became the first music video from the 1980s to pass two billion views on YouTube.

## My Reaction
"Take on Me" is pure 80s joy. The synth hook is instantly recognizable and Morten Harket's soaring falsetto in the chorus is something few singers could pull off. Pair that with a video that still looks magical today and you have a true classic.
