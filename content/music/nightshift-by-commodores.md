## About This Song
"Nightshift" is the title track of the Commodores' 1985 album. Lead singer Walter Orange wrote it with Dennis Lambert and Franne Golde as a tribute to soul and R&B greats Jackie Wilson and Marvin Gaye, who both died in 1984. Recorded that same year, it was released by Motown Records in January 1985 as the album's first single and became the group's first hit since Lionel Richie left. Bruce Springsteen later covered it on his 2022 album Only the Strong Survive.
