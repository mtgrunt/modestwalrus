## Why I Reacted To This Song
I felt this one in my bones. Relationships are inherently troubled and the song never softens that truth, rendering their messiness with the same weight they carry in real life.

## About This Song
"A Decade Under the Influence" comes from Taking Back Sunday's 2004 album *Where You Want to Be*, the first record to feature Fred Mascherino on guitar and co-lead vocals after founding members John Nolan and Shaun Cooper departed to form Straylight Run. The interplay between Fred Mascherino and Adam Lazzara's vocals became a defining trait of this stretch of the band's catalog and this song is one of the clearest showcases of it.

## First Impressions
The back-and-forth vocals grab you immediately; it feels less like a duet and more like an actual argument set to music.

## My Reaction
The push and pull between the two voices captures how messy relationships actually get in a way a single vocalist never could. Then there's the ending, Fred screaming "I got it back," which lands like a genuine release after everything that came before it. It's the kind of catharsis that only works because the song earned it.
