## Why I Reacted To This Song
Bob Dylan loved Jimi's version so much that after Hendrix died he began performing the song Jimi's way. In the liner notes to his 1985 collection Biograph, Dylan said that every time he sings it, it feels like a tribute to Hendrix. That's a humble gesture from the legend who wrote it.

## About This Song
"All Along The Watchtower" was written by Bob Dylan and first appeared on his 1967 album John Wesley Harding. The Jimi Hendrix Experience recorded their version the following year for Electric Ladyland (1968). Hendrix transformed Dylan's sparse acoustic song into an electric epic with layered guitar solos and it went on to become one of his best known recordings.

## First Impressions
The opening strum sets an ominous mood and then Jimi's guitar takes off.

## My Reaction
Hendrix didn't just cover "All Along The Watchtower," he reinvented it. Every solo builds on the last and his guitar sounds like it's telling the story right alongside the lyrics. It's rare for a cover to outshine the original, but this one did and even Dylan took it to heart.
