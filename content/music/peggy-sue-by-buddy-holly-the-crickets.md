## About This Song
"Peggy Sue" was recorded in 1957 with producer Norman Petty in Clovis, New Mexico, with The Crickets backing Buddy Holly. The song was first called "Cindy Lou" but was renamed after Peggy Sue Gerron, the girlfriend of Crickets drummer Jerry Allison. Allison's nonstop rolling drum pattern drives the whole track and it became one of Holly's biggest hits.

## First Impressions
Those rolling drums kick in right away and Buddy's hiccuping vocals make it instantly recognizable.

## My Reaction
"Peggy Sue" is simple, energetic and full of charm. The drums never let up, the guitar is crisp and Buddy's playful voice ties it all together. It's easy to hear why so many later rock musicians pointed to Buddy Holly as an influence.
