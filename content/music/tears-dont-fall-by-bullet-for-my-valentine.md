## About This Song
"Tears Don't Fall" was released in 2005 on Bullet For My Valentine's debut album, *The Poison*. It became one of the band's signature songs, built around a driving metalcore sound that helped define the UK metal scene of the mid-2000s.

## First Impressions
The double-bass drumming hits immediately and sets a relentless pace that never really lets up. The guitar work layered over it is dense and demanding, clearly built to push both players and listeners.

## My Reaction
What stands out most is how the rhythm section and guitars push each other. The double-bass drumming is epic, driving the song with a pace that feels unstoppable, while the guitar parts are magnificent and genuinely challenging. Full of intricate riffing that never sacrifices the song's hooks for technicality. It's a track that rewards paying close attention to every layer.
