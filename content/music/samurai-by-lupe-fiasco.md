## About This Song
"Samurai" is the title track and lead single from Lupe Fiasco's ninth studio album, Samurai, released in 2024. Like the rest of the album, it was produced by longtime collaborator Soundtrakk, the producer behind classics like "Kick, Push" and "Superstar." The album imagines Amy Winehouse as a battle rapper and Lupe builds the song around that vision.

## First Impressions
The jazzy, laid-back beat pulls you in and Lupe's wordplay takes it from there.

## My Reaction
"Samurai" shows Lupe Fiasco is still one of the sharpest lyricists in hip-hop. Reuniting with Soundtrakk gives the song a smooth, familiar warmth and the concept behind it makes it even more interesting to dig into.
