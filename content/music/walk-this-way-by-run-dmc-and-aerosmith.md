## About This Song
"Walk This Way" was originally a 1975 hit for Aerosmith on their album Toys in the Attic. In 1986 Run-DMC remade it for their album Raising Hell, produced by Rick Rubin, with Aerosmith's Steven Tyler and Joe Perry joining them on the track. It became a top 5 hit on the Billboard Hot 100, brought hip-hop to a huge new audience and helped revive Aerosmith's career.

## First Impressions

## My Reaction
"Walk This Way" proved rap and rock could share the same stage. And no matter what anyone says, the video still holds up today. Watching Steven Tyler break through the wall between the two bands is one of the most memorable moments in music video history.
