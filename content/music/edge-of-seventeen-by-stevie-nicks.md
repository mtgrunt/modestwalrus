## About This Song
"Edge of Seventeen," written by Stevie Nicks, appeared on her 1981 solo debut *Bella Donna*. She wrote it while grappling with innocence and loss, following the deaths of her uncle and John Lennon in close succession. The line "He seemed brokenhearted" refers to Tom Petty, though; Nicks has said it came from conversations with Petty's wife Jane, who confided in her about the state of their relationship, and the song ended up chronicling what Jane told her.

## First Impressions
That guitar figure locks in immediately and doesn't let go; it carries a tension that matches the weight of what she's singing about.

## My Reaction
Knowing the layers behind the lyrics changes how the song hits. What sounds like one grief on the surface is really several tangled together: her own losses, and someone else's heartbreak she was entrusted with. Nicks holds all of it in her voice without ever over-explaining it, which is part of what makes the song endure.
