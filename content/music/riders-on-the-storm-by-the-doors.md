## About This Song
"Riders on the Storm" closes L.A. Woman, The Doors' 1971 album, and was the last song the band recorded with Jim Morrison before his death that same year. Ray Manzarek's Fender Rhodes electric piano drifts over the sound of falling rain and rolling thunder, while Morrison sings and whispers his way through a moody, cinematic story.

## First Impressions
The rain and thunder set the scene before the first note and the mood never lets up.

## My Reaction
"Riders on the Storm" feels like a dream you can't quite shake. Manzarek's keys are hypnotic and Morrison's voice is calm yet haunting. Knowing it was one of the last things he recorded makes it hit even harder.
