## About This Song
"This Must Be the Place (Naïve Melody)" is the closing track on Speaking in Tongues, Talking Heads' fifth studio album, released in 1983. David Byrne wrote the lyrics, while the music was written by Byrne, Chris Frantz, Tina Weymouth and Jerry Harrison. Byrne called it "a love song made up almost completely of non sequiturs," written to avoid the usual clichés. The "Naïve Melody" in the title refers to its simple construction, with the guitar and bass each repeating one pattern for the whole song. It was released as a single in November 1983.

## First Impressions
The bright, looping melody feels like coming home and Byrne's vocals are unusually warm and tender.

## My Reaction
"This Must Be the Place" is one of the sweetest songs Talking Heads ever made. It trades the band's usual nervous energy for something calm and comforting. Every listen feels like a reminder to slow down and appreciate where you are and who you're with.
