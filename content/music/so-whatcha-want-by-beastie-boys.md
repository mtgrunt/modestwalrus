## About This Song
"So What'Cha Want" appears on Check Your Head, the Beastie Boys' 1992 album produced by the band with Mario Caldato Jr. Ad-Rock, Mike D and MCA trade rhymes over a heavy, organ-laced beat, with their vocals run through distortion for a raw, gritty sound. It became one of the standout tracks of the band's 90s run.

## First Impressions
The beat drops in heavy and the distorted vocals give it an edge right from the start.

## My Reaction
"So What'Cha Want" is the Beastie Boys at their loosest and most confident. The three of them bounce off each other perfectly and the fuzzy, lo-fi sound makes it stand out from anything else in hip-hop at the time. Decades later it hasn't lost a step.
