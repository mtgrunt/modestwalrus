## About This Song
"End of Beginning" is a song by Djo, the stage name of actor and musician Joe Keery, best known as Steve Harrington in Stranger Things. It appears on Decide, his second studio album, released on 16 September, 2022. Keery wrote the song and produced it with Adam Thein and it's about the recurring feeling of returning to Chicago. In 2024 TikTok users began using one of its lyrics over clips of their hometowns, which pushed it to number 11 on the Billboard Hot 100 and number 4 in the UK. It surged again in January 2026 after the Stranger Things series finale, reaching number 6 on the Hot 100 and topping the UK Singles Chart and the Billboard Global 200.

## First Impressions
The warm synths and steady guitar riff give it a dreamy, nostalgic feel right from the start.

## My Reaction
"End of Beginning" is a perfect blend of nostalgia and catchy indie pop. Keery's vocals feel honest and a little wistful and the chorus is the kind that sticks with you for days. It's easy to see why so many people connected it with their own hometowns.
