## About This Song
"Super Fly" is the title song from Super Fly, the soundtrack Curtis Mayfield wrote and produced for the 1972 film of the same name, directed by Gordon Parks Jr. The album came out on Mayfield's own label Curtom Records and hit number one on the Billboard album chart. Released as a single in October 1972, "Super Fly" reached number 8 on the Billboard Hot 100 and number 5 on the soul singles chart and was certified gold. It plays over the film's closing credits and has been sampled by artists like the Beastie Boys and The Notorious B.I.G. In 2019 the Super Fly album was added to the Library of Congress' National Recording Registry.
