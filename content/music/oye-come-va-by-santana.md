## About This Song
"Oye Como Va" is a Latin-rock reworking of a 1962 song written by Tito Puente. Santana recorded it for their 1970 album *Abraxas*, and it became one of the band's signature tracks. Carlos Santana grew up in San Francisco, and the song carries that mix of Latin rhythm and West Coast rock.

## First Impressions
The groove locks in right away. The percussion and organ set the pace, and the guitar comes in smooth and confident.

## My Reaction
The song never feels rushed. The rhythm section does the heavy lifting, and Santana's guitar sings over it with a tone that's instantly recognizable. It honors Puente's writing while making it feel completely new.
