## About This Song
"Sunshine Of Your Love" appears on Disraeli Gears, Cream's 1967 album. The power trio of Eric Clapton, Jack Bruce and Ginger Baker built it around a heavy, bluesy riff that Bruce came up with on bass. Written by Bruce, lyricist Pete Brown and Clapton, it became one of the band's biggest hits and one of the defining rock songs of the late 60s.

## First Impressions
That riff hits right away and it's impossible to mistake it for anything else.

## My Reaction
"Sunshine Of Your Love" is pure power. The riff is heavy and hypnotic, Ginger Baker's drumming gives it a rolling swing and Clapton's solo soars over the top. It's a perfect example of what made Cream one of the greatest bands of their era.
