## Game/Publisher/Developer
Doom, id Software, id Software. Released 10 December, 1993.

## My Reaction
Doom was absolutely fantastic, the kind of game that grabs you from the very first level and doesn't let go until the last demon falls.

## Overview
Doom is a first-person shooter developed and published by id Software for MS-DOS. You play as a lone space marine stationed on the moons of Mars, fighting your way through hordes of demons after an experiment goes horribly wrong. Its fast action, 3D-style levels, deathmatch multiplayer and support for player-made levels helped turn the first-person shooter into one of gaming's biggest genres.

## What I Liked
- Fast, frantic combat that still feels great to play.
- An iconic arsenal, from the shotgun to the chainsaw to the BFG.
- Levels full of secrets, keycards and surprises.
- A heavy, driving soundtrack that keeps the adrenaline pumping.

## Verdict
Doom is a landmark game that changed the industry. Decades later it's still one of the most fun shooters ever made.

## Rating
87/100
