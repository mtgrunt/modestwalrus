## My Reaction
The graphics were lit in 2013. Saints Row IV leaned all the way into the absurd and never once apologized for it.

## Overview
Saints Row IV picks up with the Saints leader now President of the United States, only for an alien race called the Zin to invade and trap everyone in a simulation of Steelport. Trapped inside, the Saints gain superpowers like super sprint, super jump and telekinesis, turning the open world into a playground for chaos rather than just a city to drive around in.

## What I Liked
- The superpowers completely change how the open world plays, making traversal as fun as any mission.
- The humor is relentless and self-aware, never taking any of its own absurdity too seriously.
- The visuals held up impressively well for 2013, especially once the simulation's digital effects kick in.

## What I Didn't Like
- Leaning this hard into superpowers and satire comes at the cost of the grounded gang-war identity earlier entries had.

## Verdict
Saints Row IV swaps grounded crime drama for full-blown absurdity and makes it work, delivering one of the most purely fun open-world games of its generation.

## Rating
82/100
