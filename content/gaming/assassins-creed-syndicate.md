## My Reaction
I was completely absorbed by this game. From the moment I set foot in Victorian London I was hooked and I kept coming back for more.

## Overview
Assassin's Creed Syndicate is an open-world action adventure developed by Ubisoft Quebec. Set in London in 1868 during the Industrial Revolution, it follows twin assassins Jacob and Evie Frye as they build a street gang and fight to free the city from Templar control. A rope launcher lets you zip across rooftops and you can commandeer carriages to race through the streets.

## What I Liked
- Victorian London is a rich, atmospheric city to explore.
- Switching between Jacob and Evie gives you two different styles of play.
- The rope launcher makes getting around fast and fun.
- Building up your gang and taking back the city's boroughs is rewarding.

## Verdict
Assassin's Creed Syndicate is an engrossing adventure that kept me invested from start to finish. It's one of the most enjoyable entries in the series.

## Rating
85/100
