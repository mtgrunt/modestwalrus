## My Reaction
The timing couldn't be better for this one. It still pulls you in with its world, its characters and its stories more than ten years after it first came out.

## Overview
The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt is an action role-playing game developed by CD Projekt Red and published by CD Projekt. You play as Geralt of Rivia, a monster hunter for hire searching for his adopted daughter Ciri, who is being chased by the Wild Hunt. The open world is packed with side quests that feel as carefully written as the main story and your choices shape how things end. It was followed by two expansions, Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine. The game later came to Nintendo Switch, received a free next-gen update in 2022 and returned in 2026 as The Witcher 3: Remastered.

## What I Liked
- Geralt is a great lead and the cast around him is full of memorable characters.
- Side quests feel like full stories of their own.
- The world is huge, beautiful and full of things to discover.
- Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine are among the best expansions ever made.

## Verdict
The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt is one of the best RPGs ever made. It's well worth playing, whether it's your first time or your fifth.
