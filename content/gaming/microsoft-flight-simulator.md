## My Reaction
Microsoft Flight Simulator is breathtaking. Taking off over a city you know and seeing it rendered below you never gets old.

## Overview
Microsoft Flight Simulator is a flight simulation game developed by Asobo Studio and published by Xbox Game Studios. It uses satellite imagery, real-world data and live weather to recreate the entire planet, so you can fly over almost any place on Earth. The 2024 edition builds on the 2020 release with a career mode, new aircraft and activities like aerial firefighting, search and rescue and crop dusting. It's available on PC and Xbox Series X|S.

## What I Liked
- The whole world is there to explore, down to your own neighborhood.
- Live weather and lighting make every flight feel different.
- Career mode and missions give you a reason to keep flying.
- It works for both casual sightseers and hardcore sim pilots.

## Verdict
Microsoft Flight Simulator is a stunning achievement that turns the planet into a playground. It's a must-play for anyone who has ever wanted to take to the skies.
