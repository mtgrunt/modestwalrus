## Game/Publisher/Developer
Brothers: A Tale of Two Sons, 505 Games, Starbreeze Studios. Released 7 August, 2013 on Xbox 360, followed by PC and PlayStation 3 on 3 September, 2013.

## My Reaction
Brothers: A Tale of Two Sons is an outstanding game. It tells a heartfelt story without a single word of real dialogue and it stays with you long after the credits roll.

## Overview
Brothers: A Tale of Two Sons is a fairy tale adventure directed by Josef Fares and developed by Starbreeze Studios. Two brothers set out on a journey to find a cure for their sick father. You control both brothers at the same time, one with each analog stick, working them together to solve puzzles and get past obstacles.

## What I Liked
- Controlling both brothers at once is a clever idea that feels fresh.
- The story is moving and told entirely through the characters' actions and gibberish speech.
- The world is beautiful and full of memorable moments.
- The puzzles are built around the two brothers working together.

## Verdict
Brothers: A Tale of Two Sons is a short but unforgettable journey. Its unique controls and emotional story make it a game well worth playing.

82/100
