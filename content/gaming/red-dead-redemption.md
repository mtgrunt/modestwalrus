## My Reaction
It's not without a few flaws, but Red Dead Redemption is very nearly the best Western game ever made, with a fantastic Old West story at its heart.

## Overview
Red Dead Redemption is a Western epic developed by Rockstar San Diego as a follow-up to their 2004 hit Red Dead Revolver. It's set at the turn of the 20th century, a time when the lawless, chaotic frontier was giving way to the growing reach of the government and the spread of the Industrial Age. You play as former outlaw John Marston, forced to hunt down the members of his old gang.

## What I Liked
- The story is gripping from start to finish with an unforgettable ending.
- The open frontier is beautiful and full of life.
- Gunfights, horseback riding and side activities capture the feel of the Old West.
- John Marston is one of gaming's great protagonists.

## Verdict
Red Dead Redemption is a landmark open-world game and a must-play for anyone who loves Westerns.
