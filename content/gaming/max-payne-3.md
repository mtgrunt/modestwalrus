## My Reaction
Max Payne 3 is such a great game and I really wish there was a new one on the way.

## Overview
Max Payne 3 is a third-person shooter published by Rockstar Games and the third game in the Max Payne series. Years after the events of the first two games, a worn-down Max takes a private security job protecting a wealthy family in São Paulo, Brazil. When things go wrong he's pulled into a violent conspiracy. The series' signature bullet time and Shootdodge moves return, letting you slow the action down and pick off enemies in style.

## What I Liked
- Bullet time and Shootdodge make every gunfight feel cinematic.
- Max's gritty, world-weary narration gives the story real character.
- The São Paulo setting is a bold, colorful change of scenery.
- The gunplay is tight, heavy and satisfying.

## Verdict
Max Payne 3 is one of the best shooters of its generation and a fitting return for Max. Here's hoping he gets another chapter someday.
