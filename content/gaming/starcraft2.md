## My Reaction
StarCraft II lived up to the legacy of the original. Every match is a test of speed, strategy and nerve and the campaigns tell an epic story across all three races.

## Overview
StarCraft II is a real-time strategy game from Blizzard Entertainment and the sequel to the hit 1998 original. Set in a militaristic far future, it tells the story of a galactic war for dominance between the Terran, Zerg and Protoss. The single-player campaign was released in three parts, each focused on one race: Wings of Liberty (2010), Heart of the Swarm (2013) and Legacy of the Void (2015). A final campaign pack, Nova Covert Ops, followed in 2016.

Its multiplayer grew into a major esports scene of its own, drawing sponsors beyond Blizzard and big audiences in South Korea and around the world, just like the original StarCraft. Since 2017 the multiplayer, co-op mode and the Wings of Liberty campaign have been free to play.

## What I Liked
- Three distinct races make every matchup feel different.
- The campaigns are packed with memorable missions and characters.
- Competitive multiplayer rewards practice and sharp decision making.
- The free-to-play option makes it easy for anyone to jump in.

## Verdict
StarCraft II is one of the finest real-time strategy games ever made. Whether you're playing the campaign or climbing the ladder, it's a game that keeps you coming back.
