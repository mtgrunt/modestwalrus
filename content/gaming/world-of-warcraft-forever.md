## My Reaction
World of Warcraft: Forever is a good experience, though it's not quite the best the game has offered.

## Overview
World of Warcraft: Forever is Blizzard's permanent "Classic+" version of World of Warcraft, announced at BlizzCon 2026. It's set during the first year of the original 2004 game and runs alongside both modern World of Warcraft and the existing Classic versions. Blizzard has added new and overhauled zones along with around 1,000 new quests across the full level 1 to 60 range, and it doesn't plan to keep raising the level cap. Access is included with a World of Warcraft subscription or game time.

## What I Liked
- Revisiting vanilla Azeroth with fresh zones and quests feels familiar yet new.
- Keeping the level cap at 60 keeps the focus on the world itself.
- It's included with a regular subscription.

## Verdict
World of Warcraft: Forever is a solid way to return to classic Azeroth. It's good, even if it doesn't quite reach the heights of the series' best.
