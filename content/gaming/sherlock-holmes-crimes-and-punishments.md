## My Reaction
Crimes & Punishments gives you plenty of ways to play detective. The investigation mechanics feel fresh and Sherlock can put on costumes and take on new characters to get what he needs. The case file system keeps every clue organized and the puzzles keep you thinking from start to finish.

## Overview
Sherlock Holmes: Crimes & Punishments is a detective adventure game developed by Frogwares. You play as Sherlock Holmes, working through a series of standalone cases with Dr. Watson at your side. You gather evidence, question suspects and piece together deductions. At the end of each case you decide who is guilty and whether to condemn or absolve them.

## What I Liked
- The new investigation mechanics make examining crime scenes feel hands-on.
- Disguising Sherlock with costumes and characters adds variety to how you gather information.
- The case file system keeps clues and leads easy to follow.
- Deductions and moral choices put the final call in your hands.
- The puzzles are varied and satisfying to solve.

## Verdict
Sherlock Holmes: Crimes & Punishments makes you feel like a real detective. Between the investigations, disguises, deductions and puzzles there's always something to figure out.
