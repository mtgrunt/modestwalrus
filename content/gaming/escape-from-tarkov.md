## My Reaction
Escape from Tarkov is tense like few other shooters. Every raid feels like a gamble and making it out alive with your loot is a rush that never gets old.

## Overview
Escape from Tarkov is a multiplayer first-person tactical extraction shooter developed and published by Battlestate Games for PC. It's set in the fictional city of Tarkov in the Norvinsk region of northwestern Russia, where two private military companies, USEC and BEAR, are at war while hostile locals called Scavs roam the streets. In each raid you hunt for loot and try to reach an extraction point before time runs out. Die and you lose everything you brought in, apart from items in your secure container or ones you insured. Between raids you build up your hideout, take on quests from traders and buy and sell gear on the flea market. After years in alpha and beta testing starting in 2016, the full 1.0 version launched on 15 November, 2025, the same day it arrived on Steam.

## What I Liked
- Every raid is high stakes, which makes every fight count.
- The gunplay and weapon customization are incredibly detailed.
- Extracting with a full bag of loot is one of the best feelings in gaming.
- The hideout, quests and flea market give you plenty to work toward between raids.

## The Controversies
- The Unheard Edition, announced in 2024, came with exclusive equipment and access to an exclusive PvE server, which critics called "pay-to-win."
- Cheating and real-money trading have been long-running problems.
- Battlestate Games has been accused of abusing YouTube's copyright takedown system against critics.

## Verdict
Escape from Tarkov is punishing, intense and deeply rewarding, even with the controversies that have followed it. It's not for everyone, but if you can handle the losses, there's nothing else quite like it.
