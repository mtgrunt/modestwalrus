## My Reaction
Even now, hearing the kraken music in the trailer has me fearing for my loot.

## Overview
Sea of Thieves is a shared-world pirate adventure developed by Rare and published by Microsoft. You set sail alone or with a crew of friends, hunting for buried treasure, raiding skeleton forts and battling sea monsters like the kraken. Other player crews roam the same waters, so any ship on the horizon could be friend or foe and your hard-earned loot is never truly safe until you cash it in.

## What I Liked
- Sailing a ship together with friends is a blast.
- Every voyage turns into its own story, from treasure hunts to surprise ambushes.
- The kraken and other sea monsters make the ocean feel dangerous.
- The bright, stylized world is beautiful to explore.

## Verdict
Sea of Thieves is one of the most fun co-op games around. Grab a crew, hoist the sails and keep an eye out for tentacles.

85/100
