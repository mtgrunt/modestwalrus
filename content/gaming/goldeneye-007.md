## My Reaction
GoldenEye 007 is one of those games that defined an era. Gathering friends around one TV for split-screen multiplayer is a memory that's hard to beat.

## Overview
GoldenEye 007 is a first-person shooter developed by Rare and published by Nintendo for the Nintendo 64. Based on the 1995 James Bond film GoldenEye, it puts you in the shoes of 007 as he works through missions full of stealth, gadgets and gunfights. Alongside the single-player campaign, its split-screen multiplayer for up to four players became legendary. The game earned widespread praise from critics and became a major commercial hit.

## What I Liked
- Split-screen multiplayer is endlessly fun with friends.
- Missions mix stealth and action with clear objectives.
- Bond's gadgets and weapons make every level feel like part of the film.
- It proved first-person shooters could work on a console.

## Verdict
GoldenEye 007 is a landmark shooter and one of the best games on the Nintendo 64. It's a true classic that shaped console gaming.
