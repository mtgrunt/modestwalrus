## My Reaction
After years of waiting and a couple of delays, Grand Theft Auto VI is finally here and it was worth the wait.

## Overview
Grand Theft Auto VI is an open-world action-adventure game developed by Rockstar North and published by Rockstar Games. It returns the series to Vice City, set within the sun-soaked fictional state of Leonida. The story follows criminal partners Lucia and Jason as they get pulled into a dangerous world of crime. It launched on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S after being pushed back from its original fall 2025 target.

## What I Liked
- Vice City and Leonida feel bigger and more alive than any Rockstar world before.
- Lucia and Jason make a compelling pair to follow.
- The level of detail in the world is staggering.

## Verdict
Grand Theft Auto VI lives up to the hype and sets a new bar for open-world games.

88/100
