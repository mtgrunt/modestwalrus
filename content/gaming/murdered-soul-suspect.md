## My Reaction
Murdered: Soul Suspect is a short game and I wasn't a fan of how quickly it was over. Just as the mystery started to pull me in, the credits were already rolling.

## Overview
Murdered: Soul Suspect is a supernatural detective game developed by Airtight Games and published by Square Enix. You play as Ronan O'Connor, a detective in Salem, Massachusetts, who is killed by a serial murderer known as the Bell Killer. Trapped between worlds as a ghost, Ronan has to gather clues, possess the living and read their thoughts to solve his own murder.

## What I Liked
- Solving your own murder as a ghost is a unique and intriguing idea.
- The spooky Salem setting fits the story well.
- Possessing people and reading their thoughts adds a fun twist to investigating.

## What I Didn't Like
- The game is short and it's over before it really gets going.

## Verdict
Murdered: Soul Suspect has a great premise, but its short length keeps it from reaching its full potential.

## Rating
79/100
