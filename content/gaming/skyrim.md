## My Reaction
Skyrim goes out of its way to make everything feel vast, from its sprawling open world to its wide-ranging skill tree.

## Overview
The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim is an open-world fantasy RPG developed by Bethesda Game Studios and published by Bethesda Softworks. Set in the snowy northern province of Skyrim, you play as the Dragonborn, a hero with the power to absorb the souls of dragons as they return to the world. Instead of picking a fixed class, your skills grow as you use them, so you can shape your character into a warrior, mage, thief or anything in between.

## What I Liked
- The open world is huge and packed with places to discover.
- Skills that improve as you use them let you play exactly how you want.
- Dragon battles and shouts make you feel truly powerful.
- Countless side quests and guilds keep you busy for hundreds of hours.

## Verdict
Skyrim is a massive adventure that rewards curiosity at every turn. It's easy to lose yourself in it for a very long time.

85/100
