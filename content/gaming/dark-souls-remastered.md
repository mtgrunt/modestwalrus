## My Reaction
In my opinion, the trailer still doesn't represent the final product very well. The footage has been darkened and saturated for a cinematic effect, which isn't what I actually saw when I played the game.

## Overview
Dark Souls: Remastered is an updated version of FromSoftware's 2011 action RPG Dark Souls, published by Bandai Namco. You play as a cursed undead exploring the crumbling kingdom of Lordran, battling fearsome enemies and massive bosses where every mistake can be deadly. The remaster brings higher resolutions, a smoother frame rate and improved online play and it includes the Artorias of the Abyss expansion.

## What I Liked
- The interconnected world of Lordran is a joy to explore and uncover.
- Tough but fair combat makes every victory feel earned.
- The boss fights are some of the most memorable in gaming.
- Smoother performance makes the classic easier to enjoy on modern systems.

## Verdict
Dark Souls: Remastered is a great way to experience a modern classic. Just don't judge it by the trailer.

81/100
