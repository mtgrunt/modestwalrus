## Game/Publisher/Developer
Street Fighter II: The World Warrior, Capcom, Capcom. Released 1991 in arcades.

## My Reaction
Street Fighter II fills me with nostalgia every time. Just hearing the music or seeing those character portraits takes me right back.

## Overview
Street Fighter II: The World Warrior is a one-on-one fighting game developed and published by Capcom, first released in arcades in 1991 and later brought to home consoles like the Super Nintendo. You choose from eight fighters from around the world, including Ryu, Ken, Chun-Li, Guile and Blanka. From there you battle your way up to the final boss, M. Bison. Its special moves and combo system set the standard for every fighting game that followed.

## What I Liked
- A memorable roster where every fighter has their own style and personality.
- Pulling off special moves like the Hadouken feels great.
- Head-to-head matches against friends are endlessly fun.
- The music and stages are instantly recognizable.

## Verdict
Street Fighter II is a true classic that defined the fighting game genre. It's pure nostalgia and still a blast to play.

## Rating
85/100
