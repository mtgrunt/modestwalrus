## My Reaction
Disco Elysium is unlike any other RPG I've played. It's sharp, strange and often hilarious and the writing pulls you in from the very first conversation.

## Overview
Disco Elysium is a role-playing game developed and published by ZA/UM. You play as a detective who wakes up with no memory in the run-down city of Revachol, where you're tasked with solving a murder alongside your calm, steady partner Kim Kitsuragi. There's no traditional combat. Instead, everything is driven by dialogue and skill checks, with your own skills acting as voices in your head that argue, advise and sometimes lead you badly astray. The Final Cut edition later added full voice acting and brought the game to consoles.

## What I Liked
- The writing is brilliant, funny and full of personality.
- Skills that talk back to you make your character's mind feel alive.
- Every choice and failed roll can take the story somewhere unexpected.
- Kim Kitsuragi is one of the best companions in gaming.

## Verdict
Disco Elysium is a bold, unforgettable RPG built on words instead of weapons. It's a must-play for anyone who loves great storytelling.
