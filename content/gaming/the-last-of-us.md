## My Reaction
The Last of Us tells one of the most powerful stories in gaming. Joel and Ellie's journey is tense, heartbreaking and unforgettable.

## Overview
The Last of Us is an action-adventure game developed by Naughty Dog and published by Sony Interactive Entertainment, led by creative director Neil Druckmann and game director Bruce Straley. It's set in a post-apocalyptic United States where a mutated Cordyceps fungus has turned much of humanity into infected. Twenty years after losing his daughter Sarah in the outbreak, smuggler Joel is tasked with escorting Ellie, a young girl who is immune to the infection, so the Fireflies can try to create a cure. The game first came out on PlayStation 3, followed by the Left Behind expansion in 2014, The Last of Us Remastered for PlayStation 4 in 2014 and the full remake, The Last of Us Part I, for PlayStation 5 in 2022 and PC in 2023. It was later adapted into an HBO series that premiered in 2023.

## What I Liked
- Joel and Ellie are one of the best duos in gaming.
- The story hits hard and the ending stays with you.
- Every encounter feels tense, with scarce supplies making every bullet count.
- The world is beautiful and haunting at the same time.

## The Controversies
- Several critics questioned whether The Last of Us Part I was needed at all, especially at its US$70 price.
- The PC version of Part I was marred by performance problems at launch and received mixed reviews.

## Verdict
The Last of Us is a masterclass in storytelling. It's a must-play for anyone who loves games with heart.

84/100
