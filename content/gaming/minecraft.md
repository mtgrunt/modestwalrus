## My Reaction
If we'd told people back then just how big this game was going to become, they never would have believed it.

## Overview
Minecraft is a sandbox game created by Markus "Notch" Persson and developed by Mojang Studios, which was later bought by Microsoft. You explore an endless world made of blocks, gathering resources, crafting tools and building anything you can imagine. Survival mode challenges you to stay alive against monsters and hunger, while Creative mode gives you unlimited blocks to build freely. It has gone on to become the best-selling video game of all time.

## What I Liked
- The freedom to build absolutely anything is endlessly creative.
- Every new world is different and full of things to discover.
- Playing with friends turns every session into a shared adventure.
- It's simple to pick up but has incredible depth.

## Verdict
Minecraft is a true phenomenon and one of the most influential games ever made. Few games have given players this much freedom.
