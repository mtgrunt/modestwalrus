## My Reaction
Madden NFL 26 brings the NFL to life once again and it's a solid addition to a long-running series.

## Overview
Madden NFL 26 is an American football video game developed by EA Orlando and published by EA Sports. Based on the National Football League, it's the 36th installment of the Madden NFL series. Philadelphia Eagles running back Saquon Barkley is on the cover. It's available on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2 and PC, making it the first Madden to leave PlayStation 4 and Xbox One behind.

## What I Liked
- The NFL presentation makes every game feel like a real broadcast.
- Up-to-date rosters keep it in step with the current season.
- Arriving on Nintendo Switch 2 gives more players a way to get in the game.

## Verdict
Madden NFL 26 is a strong entry in the series and a must for NFL fans.

84/100
