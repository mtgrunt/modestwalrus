## My Reaction
It was so legitimate. Liberty City felt alive in a way earlier entries hadn't quite managed and just cruising around causing chaos or chasing down missions never got old.

## Overview
Grand Theft Auto IV follows Niko Bellic, an Eastern European immigrant with a dark past who arrives in Liberty City chasing the American Dream his cousin Roman sold him on. Instead he finds a city run by corrupt cops, loan sharks and gangsters and gets pulled into a life of crime while working through his own history of betrayal and violence. Rockstar North rebuilt Liberty City with a heavier, grittier tone than the series had shown before, backed by a new physics engine and a more grounded approach to driving and combat.

## What I Liked
- Liberty City is dense, detailed and genuinely fun to just drive around in.
- Niko is a more grounded, complex protagonist than the series had offered before.
- The physics and driving model add a weight to the world that earlier entries lacked.

## What I Didn't Like
- Some of the mission structure leans repetitive, with a lot of drive-here-shoot-that pacing.

## Verdict
Grand Theft Auto IV is a genuinely fun ride through one of the series' best-realized cities, even if the mission variety doesn't always match the ambition of the world around it.

83/100
